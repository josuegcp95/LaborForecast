# Labor Forecast

[![Click here to download](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/82785695/200953066-c30fc85b-fded-45db-a52a-5d2baa04c5f0.png)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/labor-forecast/id6760671733)

---

##### Built with Swift and SwiftUI to explore AI exposure risk across every major US occupation:
##### • Follows MVVM architecture
##### • Scores 341 Bureau of Labor Statistics-tracked occupations 0–10 for AI exposure
##### • Browse all occupations, filter by risk level, salary, education, or industry category
##### • Check your own career's AI exposure score instantly under My Risk
##### • Discover safer career alternatives with similar salaries
##### • Save and reorder careers to a personal watchlist using AppStorage
##### • Supports light and dark mode
##### • All data bundled locally — no backend, no network calls, no authentication required

---

##### Data & Acknowledgements:
##### • Occupational data from the BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook 2024–2034
##### • Scoring methodology by Andrej Karpathy
##### • Scores are estimates, not precise predictions. Use as a starting point for career research.
